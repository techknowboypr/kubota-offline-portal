RELIABLE E-TVCS DIESEL ENGINE

Exceptionally durable and reliable engine manufactured by Kubota in Japan. Quiet, low-vibration engine featuring high power, high torque with clean emissions.

2.1m TURNING RADIUS

With the turning radius for the A211S being as tight as 2.1 m, movements in narrow places or between ridges are completed smoothly without any damage to crops.

Intergral Power Steering

Integrated efficient design provides ease of steering and low maintenance.

SUPER DRAFT CONTROL

This function contributes to a reduction of slippage when using such implements as cultivators that require strong traction. An additional advantage is that it is never necessary to adjust the depth of cultivation with the result that makes operations easier and more efficient.

ECO PTO

ECO-PTO is the function designed to perform tractor operation by maintaining the PTO revolution at 540 rpm by lowering the engine revolution from 2 600 rpm to 1 830 rpm. This phenomenon helps to reduce the fuel consumption by operating the 540 rpm of PTO at less engine rpm. This function is recommended for operations in a field of small load fluctuations or for operations of relatively small loads.
