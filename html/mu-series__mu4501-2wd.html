POMPA LIFT

Presenting the all new farmer friendly Japanese designed hydraulics with Pompa Lift that offers easy operation of hydraulic lifting with single touch operation of lever and comes with lift lock. Lift height limiter feature provides freedom of operation as per implement & soil requirement & helps reduces the fatigue on the operator by minimizing frequent manual interventions.

KQ4P ENGINE

MU4501 is equipped with Kubota Quad 4 Piston (KQ4P) engine. An extremely powerful, high fuel efficient 4 cylinder engine with a narrow piston ring which reduces friction and ultimately improves fuel efficiency.

BALANCER SHAFT

Two balancer shafts which rotates twice the engine speed to reduce overall vibration and noise of the engine.

SYNCHROMESH TRANSMISSION

MU4501 2WD is equipped with synchromesh main gear box in which synchronizer unit is provided for shifting instead of collar which results in less noise while shifting the gear. Smooth transmission of the gears also reduces wear and tear.
